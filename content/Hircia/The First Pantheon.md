The First Pantheon was the set of gods who existed and walked [[Hircia]] before [[The Divine War]]. Following [[the divine war]], they were banished beyond the material plane.

The gods in the First Pantheon include:
- **[[Visil]] aka [[The Matron]] (the Goddess of Magic & Fate):** Visil orchestrated the weakening of planar boundaries across [[Hircia]] by manipulating the Lich [[Surzak]], aiming to shatter the ancient cages keeping her trapped so she could physically walk the material world once more.
- **[[Letheon]], the God of Life & Death:** One of the most powerful and ancient of the old gods, possessing absolute control over mortality itself, holding the divine power to gift and strip away life at a whim.
- **[[Carnifex]], the God of War:** The primordial embodiment of conflict, destruction, and martial force.
- **[[Arator]], the God of Earth and Growth:** The primordial architect who physically shaped the unbroken continent of Hircia and engineered all biological life upon it.
- [[Mechanos]], the God of Order and Innovation: The deity of architecture, metalworking, and cosmic laws. He believed magic should be bound by logic, geometry, and equations rather than raw emotion. His left-behind blueprints, forgotten geometric shrines, and divine equations when found are what kick-started the rapid expansion and scientific development of [[Jotunheimr]].
- [[Janura]], the Goddess of The Spaces Between: The planar orchestrator, possessing the absolute ability to step between worlds. She was the one who eventually locked all the competing gods away at the cost of her own freedom, sacrificing herself to close the rifts and bring an end to [[The Divine War]].
- [[Amoria]], the Goddess of Beauty and Devotion: The divine inspiration of Hircia. She moves mortals to create great art, find passion, and celebrate life, ensuring the continuation of the mortal races.
### Key Figures Affected by the Pantheon
- **[[Anhural]]:** once mortal, Anhural had the audacity to challenge the absolute authority of the heavens, and [[Letheon]] in particular. He was deified by [[Letheon]] and sentenced to immortality as a punishment for daring to defy him, and banished eternally to the Plane of Death, setting the stage for [[The Festival of the Dead]].

Unless otherwise specified, these gods continue to exist post-war, but in a weakened state and mostly sealed away from mortals.
### The Origin of the Cataclysm
[[Visil]], [[Carnifex]], and [[Arator]] formed a desperate, cataclysmic alliance that initiated [[The Divine War]]. The ensuing magical and physical fallout that beheld [[Hircia]] rearranged the cosmos, ultimately resulting in the formation of the three primary [[Moons of Hircia|moons]] named after them and their ancient roles in the pantheon: **Morta, Straxus, and Cultor**.
