> *"They came to Roseport expecting to slaughter hedge mages and scholars. Instead, they were met by the roar of Pinapota artillery and the cold steel of Bunelia. The Hegemony died that day; they just didn't know it yet."* - Unknown Errun Historian

**The Great War** was a catastrophic, multi-generational global conflict that fundamentally reshaped the geopolitical landscape of [[Hircia]]. Sparked in the winter of 537 AO, the war saw the complete eradication of the ancient [[The Hegemony of Aethelgard|Hegemony of Aethelgard]] and paved the way for the modern superpowers of the world, most notably  [[the Kingdom of Errun]] and [[the Duchy of Bunelia]].

---

## The Pre-War Landscape (Circa 536)
Prior to the outbreak of the war, the world was dominated by the old guard of empires, heavily reliant on magical aristocracy and strict trade monopolies.

* [[The Hegemony of Aethelgard]]: The unquestioned masters of the Central Landmasses. An ancient, bloated empire of aristocratic mages, they strictly controlled all magical expression. They suppressed the free-form arcanists of [[Roseport]] and hoarded the technological advancements of the scientists in [[Pinapota]].
* The Errun Resistance: Not yet a kingdom, "Errun" was a desperate, underground coalition of resistance fighters trying to marry Pinapotan science with wild magic to overthrow the Hegemony.
* [[The Duchy of Bunelia]]: In [[the Gem of the West]], the Duchy was incredibly wealthy and ambitious, but heavily frustrated by Aethelgard’s crippling taxation on their gem and iron trade.
* [[The Empire of Kelpis]]: A conservative, established empire in the west that relied on its alliance with Aethelgard to keep Bunelia’s growing power in check. 

---

## The Spark: The Roseport Rebellion (Winter 537)

The tension reached a boiling point in the winter of 537. Aethelgard's inquisitors marched on [[Roseport]] with orders to execute a prominent group of rebel mages—the early founders of the Errun movement. 

Knowing they lacked the steel and military might to survive the purge, the Errun resistance sent a desperate plea across the sea to [[the Duchy of Bunelia]]. Seeing a golden opportunity to break Aethelgard's global monopoly, Bunelia answered. Utilizing the thick fogs and the navigational expertise of their navy, Bunelia secretly moved a massive fleet of warships, weapons, and raw iron into Roseport harbor.

When the Aethelgard forces arrived, they were caught completely off guard, walking into a devastating crossfire of Bunelian steel and experimental [[Pinapota]] artillery. 

---

## The Escalation
Outraged by the funding of a "terrorist rebellion," the Hegemony of Aethelgard declared total war on [[the Duchy of Bunelia]]. Aethelgard called upon their allies, [[the Empire of Kelpis]], to invade Bunelia from the south. 

Simultaneously, [[the Dominion of Savaria]] and [[the Principality of Sal]] in [[the Northern Wilds]] were dragged into the conflict, hired to secure the northern seas and provide raw lumber for the staggering naval fleets required to sustain the war.

The conflict quickly spiraled into a multi-generational meat grinder, featuring widespread magical warfare, massive naval blockades, and grueling trench combat.

---

## Aftermath and Legacy

By the time the dust settled decades later, the map of [[Hircia]] had been completely rewritten.
### The Fall of the Hegemony
[[The Hegemony of Aethelgard]] was utterly eradicated. Their grand archives and floating keeps were brought down in ruin. To this day, scavengers occasionally find remnants of their lost arcane hoard. 
### The Rise of Errun
The victorious resistance officially unified the Central Landmasses under a new banner, establishing [[the Kingdom of Errun]]. Vowing never to let mages rule unchecked again, they structured a new society balancing the utilitarian science of [[Pinapota]] and the expressive freedom of [[Roseport]].
### The Grand Alliance
Bound by decades of shared sacrifice and shed blood, [[the Kingdom of Errun]] and [[the Duchy of Bunelia]] formed an unshakable alliance. Errun emerged as the primary global power, while Bunelia secured absolute dominance over [[the Gem of the West]]. This massive shift in power paved the way for Bunelia's eventual terrifying religious rise under [[The Matron]] (649 AO).
### The Bitter Losers
[[The Empire of Kelpis]] was left humiliated, economically crippled, and deeply bitter. This resentment cemented their modern, enduring rivalry with Bunelia.

Many of the once epic Bunelian naval forces found themselves unsupported after the war concluded and many fractured to [[the Singualian Territories]] and the lawless pirate havens known today, as the wartime smuggler networks turned to permanent piracy.
