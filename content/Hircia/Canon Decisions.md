The [[Hircia#Timeline]] should be a pretty good foundation of what is canon.
- **Anything** the players experience during the campaign becomes canon, and should superseed content that may have been planned in the wiki or documented for the world.

The [[Epilogue]] is also considered a canon telling of events, albeit with flavour that comes with tales passed down generations. But fundamentally it is accurate in the following:
- The convergence occurred post [[The Isles of Fate]]
- [[The Duchy of Bunelia|Bunelia]] became a massive political powerhouse making use of some of the convergence beasts and 'aligning' with the Matron/Visil
- Some of the convergence beasts are hard to contain
