## The God of Order and Innovation
Mechanos is the deity of architecture, metalworking, cosmic structures, and absolute logic within [[The First Pantheon]]. Possessing staggering, absolute knowledge of the fundamental underpinnings of the universe, Mechanos holds the unique ability to define, catalog, and manipulate the magic of [[Hircia]] using strict reason, geometry, and equations. He rejects the concept of magic as a force of raw emotion, viewing it instead as a precise, universal science.
### The Divine Engineer and Mortals
Unlike many of the detached or tyrannical deities of the [[Pre-Dawn]] era, Mechanos worked directly with mortals on numerous occasions. He believed that the power of the cosmos should not be gatekept by birthright, and he designed enormous, intricate arcane machines that allowed ordinary mortals to harness and utilise magic, even if they completely lacked innate magical talent. Because of his foundational gifts of craftsmanship and design, many secret circles of religious artificers and master engineers still consider him their ultimate patron deity.

### The Fall and the Legacy of Jotunheimr
During the catastrophic climax of [[The Divine War]], Mechanos's unyielding logic made him a primary target for the rogue alliance. [[Carnifex]] systematically shattered his geometric shrines while [[Visil]] unraveled his perfect mathematical equations, throwing the world's magic into a volatile, chaotic state.

While the vast majority of his sprawling, planet-spanning installations and automatons were entirely vaporised or crushed during the war, some hidden vaults, blueprints, and fragmented ruins persisted beneath the earth. Centuries later, these buried caches of divine equations and mechanical remains were uncovered by the early settlers of [[Jotunheimr]]. Reconstructing Mechanos's lost principles of logical magic is exactly what instigated their sudden, rapid technological expansion, directly laying the groundwork for the towering magitech empire of giants that would flourish until 492 AO.
