## The God of Life & Death

Letheon is one of the oldest, most absolute, and formidable entities within [[The First Pantheon]]. Holding absolute dominion over mortality itself, he possesses the ultimate authority to grant or revoke life, death, and immortality at his whim. Unlike the chaotic deities of the rogue alliance, Letheon represents an unyielding, cold cosmic law.
### The Cursing of Anhural
During the height of [[The Divine War]], a mortal scholar named [[Anhural]] refused to accept the passing of his brother, whose life had been taken by Letheon's inescapable design. Fuelled by grief and hubris, [[Anhural]] managed to do what no mortal had ever achieved: he successfully navigated a path directly into Letheon’s absolute realm of death to confront the god face-to-face.

As a demonstration of ultimate spite and absolute revenge, Letheon did not execute the intruder. Instead, he punished [[Anhural]] by stripping away his mortal release, cursing him with an agonising, eternal immortality. He then permanently trapped the newly deified scholar within the depths of the death realm, setting the tragic stage centuries later for [[The Festival of the Dead]].
### The Fracturing of Reality
[[Anhural]]’s unauthorised intrusion into the realm of death had catastrophic cosmic consequences. The act of a mortal forcefully breaching the veil is believed to be the first major event that structurally weakened Hircia's planar boundaries. This initial tear left reality permanently fractured, creating the fundamental vulnerabilities that remained exploitable even after [[Janura]] sacrificed herself to [[The Divine War#The Climax Janura ’s Sacrifice (Year 0 AO)|seal the boundaries forever]].
