## The God of Earth and Growth
Arator is the primordial architect of [[The First Pantheon]] who physically shaped the unbroken continent of [[Hircia]] and engineered all biological life upon it. Alongside [[Visil]] (The Matron) and [[Carnifex]] (The Warrior), Arator formed the desperate, rogue alliance that initiated [[The Divine War]] in a bid to overthrow [[Letheon]]'s absolute laws of mortality.

Before the war, Arator's ability to shape the earth and biological forms was perfectly regulated by the cosmic equations of [[Mechanos]], the God of Order. Under those rigid laws, Arator's creations were stable, symmetric, and predictable.

However, following the deliberate defeat of Mechanos during the war's initial phase— [[The Divine War#1. The Breaking of Logic]], the absolute rules governing reality unraveled. With Mechanos’s cosmic equations shattered, Arator’s divine power over flesh, earth, and growth became wildly volatile, volatile, and entirely unpredictable. Unshackled from logic, Arator began crafting his most legendary and terrifying creations: colossal, city-sized biological beasts. These massive titans, forged from mutated earth and living flesh, were unleashed directly onto the battlefields of [[The Divine War]] to fight on behalf of the rogue alliance.

### Post-War and Post-Convergence Legacy
While many of Arator's titanic beasts were slain during the war or sealed away when [[Janura]] slammed the planar gates shut at Year 0, several of these dormant, ancient giants persisted deep beneath the earth or beneath the dark depths of [[Hircia]]'s oceans. 

Following the catastrophic events of [[The Convergence]] in 649 AO—where the planar boundaries collapsed once again—some of these slumbering primordial giants have begun to wake. These colossal beasts now wander the fractured archipelagos of the post-Convergence world, existing as living, highly volatile monuments to Arator's untamed power. (see [[Epilogue]])
