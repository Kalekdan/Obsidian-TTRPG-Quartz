## Planar Structure & Architecture

- **The Great Material Disk:** A massive physical plane containing all material stellar bodies. Rather than a vast 3D void, the material universe functions as a colossal macro-disk upon which distinct planetary systems (sub-disks) rest side-by-side.
    - **System Disks:** Individual star and planetary systems exist as localised sub-disks across the Great Material Disk. Examples include:
        - **The Hircia System:** The home system containing the planet [[Hircia]].
        - **The Koren System:** A neighbouring material system resting on the same disk plane.
- **The Parallel Planes (The Planar Loom):** Higher and lower realms of existence (e.g., celestial, elemental, infernal, or ethereal planes) exist as parallel layers floating directly above or below the Great Material Disk. Together, these upper and lower ethereal structures make up the **Planar Loom**.
- **Planar Punctures & Convergence:** When cosmic events disrupt the boundary between layers, such as [[The Convergence]] on [[Hircia]], a breach tears through the fabric of reality. These manifest as violent Planar Punctures, vertical rift spikes that pierce through the layers of the Planar Loom and directly strike a material system.
## Divine Hierarchy & Fundamental Entities

- [[Sovereign Primordials]]: Gods and divine beings whose power and domain are localised to specific planetary systems.    
    - _Example:_ [[The First Pantheon]], who rule exclusively within the Hircia System.
- [[Primordial Constants]]: Cosmic entities that exist beyond regional boundaries or individual planar layers. Bound to no single realm, they operate alongside the fundamental laws of physics across the entirety of **[NAME TBC]**.
    - _Example:_ [[Letheon]], an entity woven directly into the foundational physics of reality.
