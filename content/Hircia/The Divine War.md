**The Divine War** is the cataclysmic global conflict that brought an end to the [[Pre-Dawn]] era of [[Hircia]] and culminated in **Year 0 AO (Anno Ocassus)**. It was fought between absolute primordial forces of [[the First Pantheon]] and a rogue alliance of deities who sought to reshape the natural and magical boundaries of the material plane.

The resulting fallout fundamentally rearranged the geography of [[Hircia]], stripped mortals of their direct connection to the gods, and transformed the cosmic landscape, leaving behind the three primary moons as permanent scars of the conflict. To modern mortals, the true history of this war is entirely lost; most believe the world simply began in the year 0 under the watch of the benevolent Church of the Trinity.
### The Catalyst: The Rogue Alliance
Before the war, [[Hircia]] existed under rigid, absolute universal laws dictated by [[Letheon]] and maintained by the mathematical order of [[Mechanos]]. Magic was tightly bound by logic, geometry, and cosmic equations. Seeking to break free from these restrictive divine laws and grasp absolute control over mortality and magic, a desperate, secret pact was formed between three powerful deities:
* **[[Visil]] ([[The Matron]]):** Seeking total control over the threads of magic and fate.
* **[[Carnifex]] (The Warrior):** Craving unbridled dominion over physical conflict and raw destruction.
* **[[Arator]] (The Farmer/Creator):** Desiring to reshape biological life and landmasses free from [[Letheon]]'s boundaries.
This alliance launched a sudden, violent rebellion against [[Letheon]] and the rest of the pantheon, plunging [[Hircia]] into a century of reality-warping warfare.
### Major Phases of the War 
#### 1. The Breaking of Logic
The alliance first struck at **[[Mechanos]], the God of Order**. [[Carnifex]] shattered his geometric shrines while [[Visil]] unraveled his cosmic equations. This broke the predictable nature of reality. Without [[Mechanos]]'s laws, magic became volatile, deeply tied to raw emotion, and capable of shifting landscapes overnight. This sudden untamed power is what allowed [[Arator]] to aggressively mutate biological life across the unbroken continent. 
#### 2. The Siege of the Veil
With the world's physics unravelling, the alliance advanced on [[Letheon]] to strip him of his absolute control over mortality. It was during this brutal, multi-decade phase that the mortal champion **[[Anhural]]** made his legendary bid to challenge [[Letheon]] directly. Though the alliance severely weakened [[Letheon]], the God of Death was powerful enough to deliver a horrific punishment to [[Anhural]]—cursing him with artificial deification and a torturous immortality before banishing him to the Plane of Death.
#### 3. The Collapse of Planar Boundaries
As the war reached its peak, the sheer volume of divine magic ripped the Material Plane open. Rifts bled raw elemental and astral energy into [[Hircia]]. Tectonic plates buckled, entire mountain ranges were levelled by [[Carnifex]]'s wrath, and reality threatened to completely dissolve into chaos.
### The Climax: [[Janura]]’s Sacrifice (Year 0 AO)
By the final year of the conflict, the Material Plane was on the verge of collapsing entirely. Realising that the war would leave nothing but a void, **[[Janura]], the Goddess of The Spaces Between**, stepped directly into the cosmic crossfire. Using her absolute authority over thresholds, [[Janura]] orchestrated a massive, reality-wide seal.
At the cost of her own physical form and freedom, she threw herself into the shifting rifts, acting as a permanent anchor. In doing so, she slammed the gates of the Material Plane shut, casting the surviving members of [[the First Pantheon]] out into distant cosmic cages and forcing them into a permanently weakened state. The precise second [[Janura]]'s seal locked into place and the skies went silent became **Year 0 AO [[Hircia#Timeline|(Anno Ocassus — The Year of the Fall)]]**.
### Post-War Fallout & Cosmic Legacy
#### The Formation of the [[Moons of Hircia|Moons]]
The immense physical and magical backwash of [[Janura]]'s ultimate seal violently calcified the lingering divine essences of the rogue alliance, casting them up into [[Hircia]]'s orbit as permanent markers:
* **[[Morta]]:** The moon formed from the fallout of **[[Visil]]**.
* **[[Straxus]]:** The moon formed from the fallout of **[[Carnifex]]**. 
* **[[Cultor]]:** The moon formed from the fallout of **[[Arator]]**.

*Note: In the modern era, the [[Church of the Trinity]] falsely teaches that these moons represent benevolent guardians (The Matron, The Warrior, and The Farmer). In truth, they are cosmic scars left by the very gods who broke the world.*
#### The Path of [[Infuscus]]
Unlike the three primary moons locked in place by the Trinity's fallout, **[[Infuscus]]** behaves erratically. It represents the calcified remains of [[Janura]]’s cosmic lock. It wanders the sky on its own loop, serving as a calendar tracker for mortals. When [[Infuscus]] aligns in specific configurations, the dimensional walls of [[Janura]]'s cage grow fractionally thinner. 
#### The Mortal Inheritance
When the dust settled in Year 0 AO, the surviving mortals (including the half-giant ancestors) crawled out of the ruins of a godless, quiet world. Finding the abandoned blueprints, equations, and shrines left behind by the vanquished **[[Mechanos]]**, they used logic, geometry, and strict arcane engineering to rebuild—directly kick-starting the hyper-advanced magitech golden age of **[[Jotunheimr]]**.
