Once a poet, Themis lived a normal life, until he was faced with a terribly tragedy. There was a huge explosion in the tavern he was staying at with his family, thought to be targeting someone in particular, but everyone in the building was killed as innocent casualties.

As he was passing beyond the mortal realm, the Raven Queen intervened. She saw a soul full of potential and a will to see justice done. She saved him beyond death, making him her servant and imbuing him with her powers.


In return, Themis was tasked with carrying out the Raven Queen's justice, and ensuring the guilty are punished, and the innocent spared. Sometimes however, the Raven Queen will overrule his judgement. In any case, Themis is the executioner.


Themis is determined to solve the crime that stole his family, as he believes that if he does so, he will have paid his debt, and hopes he can see his family again.
