The Koren System is a material system within [[The Stratum]], resting as a neighbouring sub-disk alongside [[the Hircia System]] on the Great Material Disk.

In contrast to the relatively traditional fantasy setting of the Hircia System, the Koren System is defined as a high-tech sci-fi/magitech realm.

The Koren System is made up of a number of planets and stars and is a much larger system than [[The Hircia System]].
