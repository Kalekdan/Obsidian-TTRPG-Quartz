The Planar Loom is the name given to the array of planes in [[the Stratum]] existing parallel to [[the Great Material Disk]]. Each plane in the Planar Loom may relate to a single parallel system in the Great Material Disk, or in some cases, may span several material systems. In these cases, this presents an opportunity (and possibly the only practical mechanism), to step between material systems (i.e., System A -> Shared plane in Planar Loom -> System B).
