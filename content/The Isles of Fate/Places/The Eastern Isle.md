The Eastern Isle is one of the four major continental landmasses of Hircia. Characterized by its rolling plains, extensive river networks, and long, jagged coastlines, it stands as a politically fragmented, highly decentralised, and maritime-focused continent.

Unlike the centralised monarchies or aggressive military empires of the central and western lands, The Eastern Isle is defined by a delicate balance of independent republics, mercantile city-states, and maritime trade networks. Prior to The Convergence (649 AO), it was widely regarded as one of the most politically stable regions in [[Hircia]], maintaining its autonomy primarily by balancing rival external superpowers against one another.

It is made up of three large political factions, the [[City State of Momalia]], [[Republic of Teshi]] and the [[Lanian Republic]].

### Key Geographical Features
- [[The Stricken Sea]]: A wild, tempestuous channel along the western and north-western shores. It is infamous for unpredictable arcane gales and deep-sea predators, including rumours of a colossal, city-sized sea titan buried beneath its depths. It is one of the primary reasons why none of the factions on the eastern isle became political superpowers - the trade and transport off the continent is challenging, or requires a huge detour to avoid the most treacherous ocean.
- The Great Eastern Delta: A sprawling network of navigable rivers that flows through the heart of the continent, feeding into the eastern ocean and providing internal trade routes between the three sovereign powers.
- The Glass Plateau: A high, wind-swept tableland along the border between Teshi and Momalia, dotted with ancient, calcified ruins from the Pre-Dawn era.
