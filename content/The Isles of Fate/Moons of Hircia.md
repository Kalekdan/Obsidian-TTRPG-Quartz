There are 3 primary moons in the night sky over [[Hircia]]. They are the inspiration for one of the primary religions in [[Hircia]] as well, the [[Church of the Trinity]]. A single smaller moon travels across the sky in a monthly pattern, and is used primarily for marking the calendars.

## Morta (the mother)
The largest of the moons in the night sky.
## Straxus (the warrior)
A smaller red moon.
## Cultor (the farmer)
A medium sized moon with a brownish hue.

## Infuscus 
A small dark rock moon around [[Hircia]]. It is often difficult to see against the dark night sky.

**The Irony of the Moons:** To the modern mortals, the Church of the Trinity preaches that the moons are benevolent guides (The Matron, Warrior, Farmer). In reality, those moons are scars left behind by the very alliance of rogue gods that caused the world to collapse in Year 0 (see [[The First Pantheon]]).
