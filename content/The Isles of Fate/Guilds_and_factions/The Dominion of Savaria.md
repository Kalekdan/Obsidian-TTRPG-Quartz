The Dominion of Savaria is one of the largest of the political powers in the Isles of Fate, due to the vast area of land it owns. The land owned (mostly on [[The Northern Wilds]]) is largely unpopulated and untraversable, but the scale alone makes Savaria a significant provider of resources, and virtually impossible for anyone to consider an invasion of.

Savaria is challenging to govern, in part due to the sheer scale of the land it covers. With dense jungle spanning much of the centre of the continent, settlements are generally smaller and fractured, and don't report in to a central 'government' type structure unlike much of the rest of [[Hircia]]. The towns and cities along the frontier of Savaria are where the majority of the people live, and provide the sources of trade and income for much of the continent.
