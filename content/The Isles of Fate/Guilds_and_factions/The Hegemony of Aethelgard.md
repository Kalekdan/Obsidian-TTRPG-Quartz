> *"Order is just tyranny with its sleeves rolled down."* - A Roseport rebel broadside, 537 AO

The Hegemony of Aethelgard was the primary ruling power in [[Hircia]] before [[The Great War]]. They were an old-fashioned aristocracy of mages, and believed in control over who else could practice magic (partly for fear of losing control).

They were fully destroyed during [[the Great War]] when their allies in [[The Kingdom of Errun|Errun]] betrayed them by joining forces with the rebels in [[The Duchy of Bunelia|Bunelia]].
