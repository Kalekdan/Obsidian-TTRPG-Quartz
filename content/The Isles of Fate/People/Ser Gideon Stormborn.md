Ser Gideon Stormborn was a member of the council of [[The Duchy of Bunelia]], who showed up seemingly out of the blue following the death of [[Duke Alosrin Eclipsus]]. He claimed to be the long lost bastard son of the duke, and rightful heir.

His mother is [[Elowa Goldleaf]], a jeweller at [[Starlit Stones Jewelers]] in [[Bres]]. 

He owns a will and testament from the duke, but this was produced under the effects of strong magic/drugs, so is innaccurate. He also owns the hereditary ring of the Eclipsus household, but this is also a very good fake, thanks to his links to the jewellery trade.
